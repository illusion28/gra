Baked Ziti

This is the best baked ziti recipe you’ve ever had! It takes a classic dish and kicks it up about ten more notches, A mixture of marinara and Alfredo sauce along with five additional cheeses make an over–the–top, flavorful pasta. And you don’t have to wait for a trip to Olive Garden to enjoy it!

Ingredients

Pasta

  • 1 pound ziti pasta
  • 4 cups marinara sauce
  • 2 cups Alfredo sauce
  • ½ cup ricotta cheese
  • ½ cup fontina cheese, shredded

Topping

  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
  • ¼ cup Romano cheese, grated
  • ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Cook the pasta on minute shy of the directions on the box and drain.
  3. In a large metal bowl add the pasta, marinara sauce, ricotta cheese, and fontina cheese and mix well.
  4. Add to a large oven safe skillet or 9×13–inch pan.
  5. Mix the mozzarella, panko, Romano, parmesan, and garlic together and add the topping over the pasta.
  6. Bake for 30–35 minutes uncoverd until golden brown and bubbly.
